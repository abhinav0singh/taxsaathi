About

Why TaxSaathi exists

Most calculators show you a number. They don't show you the New Regime's rebate WALL — the zone just past ₹12L taxable income where marginal relief means almost every extra rupee you earn costs almost a full rupee in tax, before easing back to normal. It's not a cliff to zero; it's a real, verifiable wall built into the tax code. TaxSaathi surfaces it directly, alongside the other signals most tools skip: how much headroom you still have on your 80C deductions, exactly how far you are from your next tax slab, and how your tax compares to what the same income would have owed before the FY2026-27 reform.

Figures shown are for FY 2026-27. There's no login and nothing about your income is stored — every calculation runs fresh, per request.

FAQ

Common questions

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. TaxSaathi doesn't require an account, and the numbers you enter aren't saved — each calculation is computed fresh and isn't retained afterward.

What exactly is the "rebate wall"?

Under the New Regime, taxable income up to ₹12L pays zero tax under Section 87A. Past that, marginal relief kicks in: your tax is capped at the amount your income exceeds ₹12L by — so at ₹12.1L, you pay roughly ₹10,000, not the full slab tax of ₹61,500. But since that cap grows ₹1-for-₹1 with your income, it means almost every extra rupee earned in this zone (up to about ₹12.7L) costs almost a full rupee in tax. It's a wall, not a cliff to zero — real, and built into the law, not a sudden drop-off.

Which regime should I choose?

It depends entirely on your actual numbers — your income, and how much you claim under 80C/80D. That's exactly what the calculator above works out for you rather than guessing; for most salaried earners without large deductions, the New Regime tends to come out ahead, but your own figures are what decide it.

Does this replace a chartered accountant?

No — TaxSaathi is a planning and awareness tool, not professional tax or legal advice. For filing, or anything with real financial stakes, check with a qualified CA.

What if the AI-phrased answers aren't showing up?

The Q&A section falls back to a plain-reference answer whenever the AI phrasing layer is unavailable — you'll still get a correct answer, just without the natural-language phrasing on top.

FAQ / Good to know

The small print,
made human.

What exactly is the “rebate wall”? ＋

Under the New Regime, taxable income up to ₹12L pays zero tax. Past that, marginal relief caps the tax increase at the amount your income exceeds ₹12L by. It is not a hard cliff to zero — it is a genuine high-friction zone where each extra rupee can cost almost a rupee in tax.

Is my income stored anywhere? ＋

No. TaxSaathi is stateless by design. Your inputs are sent for the calculation request and kept only in this browser session for the screen you are using.

Does this replace a Chartered Accountant? ＋

No. This is an educational planning tool, not professional tax or legal advice. For filing or anything with real financial stakes, verify the result with a qualified CA.
